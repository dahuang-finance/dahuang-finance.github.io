Fonts in this folder
====================

  xcharter-roman.woff2
  xcharter-italic.woff2
  xcharter-bold.woff2
  xcharter-bolditalic.woff2

These files are subsets (Latin characters only), in WOFF2 format, of the
XCharter OpenType fonts, version 1.221, by Michael Sharpe. XCharter is an
extension of Bitstream Charter. The files were made with fontTools from the
XCharter package on CTAN (https://ctan.org/pkg/xcharter). The outlines are
unchanged, and each file has its source font's copyright notice in its
name table.
